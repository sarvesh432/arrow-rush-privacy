Arrow Rush - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Sarvesh Enterprises ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Arrow Rush mobile game ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Arrow Rush.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Rush does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, phone number, or email address in order to play the game.

The App may automatically process certain technical information, such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interaction information, and diagnostic information, where required by third-party advertising services.

2. Advertising

Arrow Rush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable advertising technologies.

For more information about how Google handles data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App and its third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the game.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.
  • Detect errors, abuse, or technical problems.
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud.

4. Third-Party Services

Arrow Rush may use third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Rush is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Arrow Rush does not require users to create an account, we generally do not maintain a personal user account that can be deleted on request.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
